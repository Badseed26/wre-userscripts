# wre-userscripts

Tampermonkey scripts for WRE. Open an install link in a browser with Tampermonkey and hit Install. Updates come through automatically: each script checks this repo every 2 min and runs a newer version on the next page load (live updates, from 23/10), and Tampermonkey's own update every few hours catches the installed copy up. A change that needs a new permission waits for that Tampermonkey update. The code is in src/, the files here are built from it by build.py.

- WRE Calendar NPT 3.2.2 (PC and iPhone): https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Badseed26/wre-userscripts/main/wre-calendar-npt.user.js
  N P T (notes, photos, timesheet) badges on each booking for the days in view: green in, red missing, orange for today (including bookings that haven't started yet), blue OK'd by you (a red letter or the dashboard's OK; clearing a lad's issue counts too, ticking off one of your own follow ups doesn't), purple when it's a shared job and a crew mate put the photos in but this person didn't (counts as in, so it's never chased, it's just there for you to see). T needs two lines per person for the day: Travel and time on site (NTT, or NTA for apprentices), notes on a line are fine. Anything OK'd keeps getting checked and goes green once it really comes in. Click or tap a booking and the popup shows the badge with the details at the top. The lads' missing ones go on the WRE daily check list in Todoist, Ethan's own go in his WRE follow ups (due the day they're added), and both update and tick off as things come in.
  Ambrose jobs get a 4th letter, A: are the photos (2 or more) or the report up on Ambrose for that booking. Each visit is matched to its Ambrose booking by PO and date, with the schedule note (iso, fit off, report) as a backup; no booking that day means no A. Orange while the lads' photos aren't in AroFlo yet or it's today, red once they are and Ambrose still hasn't got them, click to OK. On the PC it checks Tradies Admin in a background tab every 2 hours (and on the header recheck), and any PO page you open there gets read too. Red ones from before today go in the Ambrose uploads section of WRE daily check, which the Desk Panel shows on its Ambrose tab. The phone shows what the PC last saw. An issue for a visit that's been moved to someone else (they're not booked on that task that day any more) gets a comment and ticked off once that day's in view.
- WRE Inbox Pull 1.6 (Outlook, PC and iPhone Safari): https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Badseed26/wre-userscripts/main/wre-inbox-pull.user.js
  Hit Pull from Outlook on the WRE Dashboard: Outlook opens, reads the day read only, sends the bundle back to the dashboard and closes. Also runs from the WRE inbox button in Outlook. - WRE Inbox Filer 0.9.74 (PC only, beta, starts in dry run): https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Badseed26/wre-userscripts/main/wre-inbox-filer.user.js
  Watches the shared Outlook for builder POs, revised POs and Energex safety advice from the senders on the Inbox filer rules task, reads them with Claude Sonnet 5.5 at high effort (repair schedule PDFs included) and files them onto the right AroFlo task: the PO plus every other PDF and photo on the email attached, new works written in Lisa's layout for the boys, ESA noted. Dates to book and anything it can't do go to the To book and Errors / to check sections of WRE follow ups. Each visit on a To book item gets a plan worked out by script (who can go by the job type, the day, start and end back to back with travel in it, about how long off the PO lines and the Ambrose slot, before or after the other trades, and anything to check), and the dashboard's Schedule job or Move job button books it in AroFlo as UNCONFIRMED through AroFlo's own schedule save. Once visits are booked the dashboard has a draft to the supervisor with our schedule, pending HO confirmation. A visit Lisa's already booked shows as Lisa done and is never touched, and the item clears once every visit has a confirmed booking. An Ambrose Schedule Update email gets its repair schedule PDF read for the electrical dates, and the job's To book item is updated or made from them. Each of those carries a link to the AroFlo task, which the dashboard shows as an Open in AroFlo button. Push to AroFlo on a captured site info item (gate code, dogs, access, site contact from your phone) puts that one line on top of the AroFlo task description, nothing else touched, checks it after the save, then comments and closes the item; if it can't, the item says why. Every hour the open jobs' phone numbers (site contact, supervisor, the task's Contact and any other) go on the * Phone list task so WRE Capture can match a note or a screenshot of a text to the job by number. A job it sets up gets its WRE job tracker card straight away (Booked / in progress), and a PO landing on a job that already has a card adds a comment to it. Note to AroFlo on a job's history line on the dashboard puts that line on the AroFlo task as an Internal Only HTML note, then marks it In AroFlo on the job; if it can't, the item lands in Errors / to check saying why. Each filer tab logs when it's been out to it: thrown out by Chrome's Memory Saver, frozen by Chrome, or nothing running for 3 min or more (PC asleep). The filer's Outlook tab always tells Outlook it's on screen, and keeps Outlook drawing while the tab's in the background (Chrome stops drawing hidden tabs, and Outlook only puts new mail in the list when it draws), and each new email's log line says how long after it came in it got picked up. The Outlook tab puts a heartbeat on the log (hourly, or straight away when something looks off) with what it can see and anything holding it back, lets go of a scan stuck over 5 min, and loads the mail page fresh if a reload never happens. Only runs in the two tabs opened from the Tampermonkey menu (Open the filer tabs), pin both and keep them open. Pause, dry run, leave dates and the sender list are on the WRE Dashboard (Inbox filer panel), with links to open the filer tabs. An updated PO gets its new lines compared with the task description by Sonnet, and the check item lists what changed (added, gone, quantities, rooms, visits) and what to fix, since the builder hardly ever says. Builder cancellations (a cancelled PO, a cash settled or closed out job) get flagged and the job's To book item closed; the email body beats any schedule PDF attached. Works across Ambrose, NthStar, Responsive, Reconstruct, Bentino and ARC (ARC tasks are named by the full PO like Lisa does). If a PO looks new but one open task already sits at that address without the PO number (unit style addresses like 42A - 42B/ count), the PO goes under that task's description with the PDF attached and the check item says so; two or more open there and it flags it instead. Quote tasks are never matched, and two open tasks for one ref is fine when only one already carries the PO number. Stops itself after 200 reads in a day; the day's reads and the month's spend show on the log. Skipped emails that carried a PDF go on one low priority item a day to eyeball. Task names stay inside AroFlo's 50 character limit (state and postcode dropped the way Lisa does it). Each filer tab keeps a tiny WebRTC link to itself so Chrome doesn't hold its timers back when it's hidden (only those two tabs, nothing leaves the PC), and the log shows whether the page timers are ok or held back. The Outlook tab reloads itself if it goes quiet for 10 min, and the log on the dashboard shows what each tab is up to. A short pause (say to install a version) carries on where it left off, only a long one skips the backlog. A second filer tab never picks up a job another tab is mid way through. Uploads follow AroFlo's own upload box state by state and only press Done on Complete; a failed one says where it got to, keeps the PDF, and Try again on the check item redoes just the upload. If one filer tab goes quiet for 8 min (closed, moved, or dropped by Chrome), the other opens it again. Uploads go through the task's own Add button on the Documents & Photos tab (the page's top menu Add puts files in company documents). Proven live 19/10: creating a task and location, the header save after appending a PO. Still to prove: the PDF upload landing on the task. The Outlook tab reloads itself every 30 min when it's idle (Outlook's list can freeze in a tab left open all day), the log shows the newest email it can see so the dashboard can flag a stuck list, and the AroFlo tab checks the session itself every 15 min so a timed out sign in gets flagged. The 30 min reload only waits for a read under way, a test, or you clicking in the tab, the tab's status shows when its page last loaded, a page that won't reload (a Leave site box) gets loaded fresh from its address, and if a reload still shows the same old newest email in work hours it loads the mail page fresh once and says so in the log. PDFs are only taken whole: one Outlook fetched in pieces gets fetched whole from the same place, a PDF Claude won't open is grabbed again once on a fresh page, then the email is read without it and the error item says what was wrong with it (cut short, password locked). Try again on a "couldn't read an email" item finds the email again and reads it.
- WRE Clipboard Brain 1.1 (AutoHotkey v2, not Tampermonkey): download wre-clipboard-brain.ahk and double click it. Select text, then Ctrl+Alt+1 PO lines for the boys, Ctrl+Alt+2 customer text in your style, Ctrl+Alt+3 promise check, Ctrl+Alt+4 tidy an invoice description into your usual invoice style. Preview before anything pastes.
- Desk Panel 2.7 (PC server for the iPad desk panel, not Tampermonkey): download the panel folder, setup steps in panel/README.txt. Dark tiles with an icon each, a Favourites tab, a Lads tab and an at-a-glance bar showing what notes, photos and timesheets are still owed (a job two lads both owe notes or photos on says who it's shared with, to match the dashboard's group text) (from the WRE Calendar NPT issues in Todoist, token saved on the PC only), where you can OK an item or tick an issue off like on the dashboard, an Ambrose tab and glance line for photos and reports still to go up on Ambrose (the calendar's A letters), open work sites, run the WRE checks through the dashboard (inbox check lets you pick the day and pulls it first), run AHK files, fire the Clipboard Brain hotkeys and lock the PC. Change any tile's icon from the iPad (pencil button) or drop images in the icons folder on the PC. Home wifi only. Updates itself from this repo every 15 minutes (panel, buttons and Clipboard Brain), so button changes go in panel/buttons.json here.

Saved settings (API key, signature, Todoist token) live in each device's Tampermonkey, never in this repo.
